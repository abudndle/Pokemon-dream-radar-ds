# Original-style D-pad Search Controls

- **Cloud search:** D-pad aims smoothly in the radar view; hold A to shoot at clouds. No joystick or extra FIRE button is drawn.
- **Outcome:** A cloud must be shot before it can yield Dream Orbs or begin a Pokémon/item encounter. The latter is resolved through the original-style capture phase and shown in research results.
- **Pokémon/item encounter:** D-pad aims and A fires; the three support-item touch controls remain enabled, and use original extracted bottom-screen artwork.
- **No camera:** The cloud fallback image remains in place on the top screen.
- **Other screens:** No joystick controls are shown.

Important: This preserves the project's existing prototype reward probabilities, which have not been proven identical to the original 3DS code.
