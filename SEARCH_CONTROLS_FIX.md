# Search control correction

The original 3DS top-screen radar cannot be directly touched on a Nintendo DS/DSi. The previous prototype accepted touchscreen aiming only at bottom-screen y<48, making most cloud targets unreachable by stylus.

This revision maps the entire bottom touchscreen to the top-screen cloud field (x is 1:1; y scales to 24..168). Hold the stylus to aim and shoot. D-pad + A works independently. During an encounter, the three lower hexagon touch zones remain reserved for support items; the upper 48-pixel strip aims/fires, or use D-pad + A. The bottom-right Back region is excluded from firing.

This is a specific control fix, not a verified reproduction of the original 3DS gyro-based aiming or an emulator-tested ROM.
